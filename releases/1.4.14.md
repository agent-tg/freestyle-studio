In Studio, select your first screen or window, then use **Video → Add Scene** to add up to three more. While recording, click a scene’s name or press **Control–Option–4** through **Control–Option–7** to cut to it.

**Control–Option–C** shows your script card and returns to the last scene. Each selected screen or window has its own full-resolution recording for editing. When system audio is enabled, it follows the primary source throughout the take.
