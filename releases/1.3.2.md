# Freestyle Studio 1.3.2

- Open the live HTML cards from the Cards menu, follow links, reveal the source file, or copy its path.
- Import scripts and transcripts directly from the Files menu.
- Keep your preferred script font, spacing, width and reading settings between launches.
- Frame the screen and camera in Freestyle. Center the screen or trim browser tabs and toolbars with adjustable edges.
- See levels for every microphone input and use the five-second speech check to set recording gain. System audio now defaults to full volume and remembers your adjustment.

Microphone gain remains under your control. Use **Set speech level…** with your normal speaking voice before recording.
