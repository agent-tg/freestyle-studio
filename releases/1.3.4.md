# Freestyle Studio 1.3.4

- Freestyle Studio now stays in the menu bar. Closing the window hides it instead of quitting the app.
- The Dock icon only shows while the window is open. Open the window again with **Show Freestyle Studio** in the menu bar.
- Quit from the menu bar or with ⌘Q.
- New **Open at Login** option in the menu bar menu starts the app in the menu bar when you log in. It is off unless you turn it on.

Requires macOS 14 or newer. Supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. Google sign-in and Drive uploads are not included in team builds.
