## What's new in 1.4.10

Safer saving. This update fixes rare cases where a good recording or your edits could be lost.

- **Takes are saved even when something small goes wrong.** If the sound took too long to finish, or an extra source file (camera, screen or mic in the `Sources` folder) did not finish, your take is still saved. A short note tells you what happened, and any unfinished file is kept so it can be recovered.
- **No more endless "Saving".** If saving gets stuck for a full minute, Freestyle Studio stops waiting, keeps a recovery file and lets you record again.
- **Quit asks before throwing away edits.** If you have unsaved script edits or an unsaved recording review, Quit and **Update** ask first. **Keep Editing** is the default.
- **Script edits stay in the right script.** While you are editing a script, you can't switch to another one. Finish or cancel your edit first.
- **Script labels stay put.** Draft, Ready and Recorded labels are no longer reset when you open a script file as the app starts.
- **Stopping right away is quiet.** Pressing Stop the moment the countdown ends no longer shows an error.
- **Recording review is steadier.** Upload, **Make AI prompt** and **Copy path** no longer trip over each other. A recording is moved into its Workspace after its AI prompt finishes.
- **Fixed:** cancelled Freestyle recordings no longer leave empty entries behind.
