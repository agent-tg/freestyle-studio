## What's new in 1.4.9

- **Cleaner menu bar menu.** Pick your microphone right from the top of the menu, start a **New Recording** in one click, or control an active recording with **Pause**, **Draw** and **Stop**. Your **Last recording** stays right there with **Copy link**, **Show in Finder**, **Make/Copy AI prompt**, and **Retry upload**.
- **Smarter readiness checks.** Closing the recorder window keeps the menu bar **Ready** while releasing your camera, microphone and screen. If a permission is off or a camera isn't sending video, the menu offers a one-click fix (**Open System Settings** or **Turn Camera off**).
- **Simpler General settings.** **Settings → General** opens in a compact window with **Appearance**, **Open at Login**, **Check for Updates…** and **Report a Problem…** up front, and expands automatically when you switch to **Workspaces**.
- **Last recording on every Freestyle tab.** Your newest saved Freestyle recording stays one click away whether you are on **Record**, **Look** or **Audio**.
- **Collapsible sidebar and wider script panel.** Click the bottom-left sidebar button to collapse controls to a slim status + Record strip, and drag the divider beside your Studio script to widen it up to half the window.
- **Separate source files for Studio takes.** Studio now saves full, uncropped camera, screen and microphone files in a `Sources` folder beside each take so you can reframe in post (toggle in **Settings → General**).
- **Fixed:** Framed layout in Freestyle no longer shows a duplicate floating camera bubble on the desktop.
