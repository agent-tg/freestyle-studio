Freestyle Studio 1.4.4 is simpler, faster and more reliable.

- **Take saved strip.** After a Studio take, a small strip stays up with Play, Record another take and More. Record another take starts a new numbered take and keeps the old one.
- **Fewer buttons.** The Freestyle Last recording card shows Play, Share, Make prompt and More. Studio tabs show one Layout row, slide options only for scripts with slides, and profile editing under Preview options.
- **Keep your share link.** After an upload, the link stays visible with Copy link and Open link.
- **Reliable Record and Stop.** Record can't start twice, Stop works right after the countdown, and the full audio is written before a take says Saved.
- **Faster.** Menus open from a cached list, the app no longer checks the takes folder twice a second, and an unchanged screen source keeps running.
- **Clearer labels.** Adjust framing, Show in Finder, and Stop scrolling at slide end. The permissions button opens the setting that is actually missing.
