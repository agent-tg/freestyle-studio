## What's new in 1.4.8

- **Framed layout.** Show your screen in a rounded window on a backdrop, with the camera in its own tile beside it. Pick it under Layout in Freestyle, or under Arrange in Studio landscape.
- **New menu bar panel.** The menu bar icon now opens a panel with status, your microphone, recent recordings and quick actions. The app shows in the Dock while the recorder window is open.
- **Spoken bullets in scripts.** A line starting with `> -` shows on the prompter as a bullet you read aloud. A stage note right before a slide shows as a small subtitle under that slide's heading.
- **Warnings clear themselves.** "Could not select microphone" and "screen paused" messages go away once the problem is fixed.
- **Simpler Audio tab.** One hint appears only when your chosen input is silent and another input has sound.
- The "Take saved" strip is one compact row and clears when you move to the next video.
- Fixed: after quitting or updating, the app could fail to reopen its window.
- AI agents can now check and fix your recording setup, and can load a script the moment it is written.
