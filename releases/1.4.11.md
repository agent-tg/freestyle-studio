Studio can now show your shared window and a script’s step chip in the same landscape take. In Framed, the camera and chip share the right column. Advancing the script updates the chip.

Press Control–Option–C to cut the large panel to the current script card. Press it again to return to your screen. The controls below the preview and the set_studio_content agent command can also make the cut.

For editing, Studio saves the full camera, shared screen, cards, step chip, and available audio as separate files beside the composed take. You can change this in Settings → General → Save separate files for editing.
