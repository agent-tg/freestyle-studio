Freestyle Studio now stays in the menu bar without a Dock icon, even while the recorder is open.

- Closing the idle recorder stops its screen, camera and microphone previews.
- Reopen it from the menu bar to start previews again.
- Open at Login keeps capture off until you open the recorder or start a recording.
- Settings is available in the menu bar menu.

macOS still shows its capture indicator during live preview or recording. Stop and save an active take before closing the recorder.
